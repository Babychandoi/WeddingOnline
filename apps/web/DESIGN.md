# Design System: DamCuoiOnline Thiệp Cưới

Scope: the online wedding invitation world-system only, which covers the public card (`/w/[slug]/invite`, short link `/thiep/[slug]`), the template demos (`/thiep-cuoi/[templateId]`), the card thumbnails in the gallery and editor picker, and the editor's live preview. The rest of the app is outside it: the gallery's own page chrome, the demo sample banner, the dashboard editor UI (`InvitationEditor`), admin, and the 3D hall each keep the app's general rose/slate Tailwind styling and are not governed here. Source of truth for every world value is `src/components/invitation/themes.ts`; the frontmatter mirrors it, keyed `<world>-<role>`.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Card You Open"**

The invitation is a printed Vietnamese wedding card, not a website. A guest receives a sealed envelope addressed to them by name; tapping breaks the seal, the flap swings back, the card rises, and the card itself becomes the page. Everything after the envelope lives on one sheet of card stock (max 560px) set on the world's ground, with its own grain, its own printed border, its own drawn ornament family and its own type pairing.

There is no single palette. The system is twelve complete paper-and-ink worlds that share one structure: the same roles (ground, paper, ink, ink-soft, accent, accent-text, on-accent, line, panel), the same section order, and the same components, re-skinned per world through `--inv-*` custom properties set on the invitation root. A world is defined by its tokens plus four structure switches: corner language (`shape`), printed card frame (`cardFrame`), countdown form (`countdown`) and photo frame (`frame`). New worlds are made by adding a theme entry, never by forking components.

The register is ceremonial and quiet: centered, generous vertical rhythm, names at full width, dates set as printed type. Guests are often older relatives on phones inside Zalo, so body text never drops below 13px, taps are at least 44 to 48px, and every face carries the Vietnamese subset.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Envelope first: a full-screen sealed envelope in the world's colours, addressed "Kính mời" + guest name (or "Quý khách"), with a wax seal at the flap.
- Twelve worlds, one structure: roles and components are shared; colour, faces, ornament, corners, frame and countdown form are per world.
- Ornaments are hand-drawn inline SVG per world (囍 seal glyph, cloud scrolls, lotus, hairline diamond, watercolour roses, eucalyptus, lace scallops, baroque scrolls, ribbon and hearts, a red masthead rule, sea waves, a single-line rose).
- Paper grain is an inline SVG feTurbulence tile, blended multiply on light paper and screen on dark paper.
- Solar date, lunar date and countdown always travel together.
- No raster imagery ships with the system. The only images are the couple's own photos, the VietQR code generated per gift, and the pre-existing 3D-hall still `/venues/<id>.webp`, rendered in-house by `scripts/render-venue-thumbnails.mjs`.

## Colors

Each world is a closed palette of nine roles. Values live in the frontmatter (`<world>-<role>`); the prose below describes the roles, not the hex values.

### Primary
- **Accent** (`<world>-accent`): the world's metal or signature ink: gold leaf on lacquer red, lotus rose, eucalyptus green, magazine red, harbour blue. Fills the primary button, selected choices, the music toggle, ornament strokes, outline-button borders, the focus ring and text selection.
- **Accent Text** (`<world>-accent-text`): the accent adjusted to read at 4.5:1 or better on paper and panel. Used for every piece of accent-coloured *text*: event titles, family headings ("Nhà Trai", "Nhà Gái"), the date numeral, countdown numerals, the "&" between names, the guest's name in the invitation wording, story dates, error and status text. Worlds whose fill accent already passes (toi-gian, tap-chi, bien-xanh, do-do) omit it and fall back to accent.
- **On Accent** (`<world>-on-accent`): text and icons on accent fills, verified at 4.5:1.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`<world>-ground`): the table the card lies on, and the envelope screen background. Always darker or more saturated than the paper so the card reads as an object.
- **Paper** (`<world>-paper`): the card stock and the page surface.
- **Panel** (`<world>-panel`): a second paper tone for fields, countdown tiles, wish cards and confirmation panels.
- **Ink** (`<world>-ink`): primary text.
- **Ink Soft** (`<world>-ink-soft`): secondary text (addresses, lunar dates, labels, captions), tinted from the world's ink rather than a stock grey. In tap-chi the ink is a neutral black, so its soft ink is a neutral dark grey by derivation.
- **Line** (`<world>-line`): hairlines, borders, the card frame, rules around date and countdown, the story timeline.

Envelope colours (body, flap, liner, seal, seal ink, address text) and petal colours are per-world values in `themes.ts` (`envelope`, `petals`); they extend the world palette into the envelope and are not reused elsewhere.

### Named Rules
**The Closed World Rule.** A world's surfaces use only that world's nine roles plus its envelope and drawn-ornament colours. Nothing borrows from another world or from the app's rose/slate chrome.

**The Readable Accent Rule.** Accent-coloured text always uses `accent-text`. The fill accent is for fills, strokes and borders; when it cannot reach 4.5:1 as text, the world defines an `accent-text`.

## Typography

**Display Font:** per world, `var(--inv-display)`: Noto Serif Display, Taviraj, Spectral, Prata, EB Garamond, Josefin Sans, Old Standard TT, Quicksand, Archivo, Be Vietnam Pro, Yeseva One (fallback Georgia serif or system-ui sans by world).
**Script Font:** per world, `var(--inv-script)`: Imperial Script, Ephesis, Moon Dance, Great Vibes, Water Brush, Corinthia, Pinyon Script, Dancing Script, The Nautigal, Alex Brush; toi-gian and tap-chi use Prata as their "script" (fallback "Segoe Script", cursive).
**Body Font:** per world, `var(--inv-body)`, usually the display face; do-do pairs Yeseva One display with EB Garamond body.

**Character:** a ceremonial display or script face for the couple's names and section titles against a readable book face, chosen per world the way a printer picks type for a card. All 21 faces are self-hosted through `next/font/google` with the `vietnamese` subset and `preload: false`, so a guest downloads only the faces their world uses.

### Hierarchy
- **Names** (script 400, clamp(3rem, 15vw, 4.6rem), 1.05; or display 600, clamp(2.6rem, 12vw, 4rem), 1, -0.01em; uppercase display clamp(2.2rem, 11vw, 3.6rem), 0.04em; masthead 900, clamp(2.3rem, 11vw, 3.4rem), 0.95, -0.03em): the couple's names, groom / "&" at 0.5em in script and accent-text / bride, each on its own line. `namesIn` picks script or display per world; single-weight display faces set `namesWeight: 400` (no faux bold).
- **Date numeral** (display 600, clamp(3rem, 15vw, 4.2rem), 1, accent-text, tabular): the day in the classic three-part date block, weekday | day | month and year, flanked by ruled cells (15px uppercase, 0.12em). Lunar date sits below in 15px italic ink-soft.
- **Section title** (script clamp(2.1rem, 8vw, 2.7rem) when the world sets names in script, otherwise display clamp(1.55rem, 6vw, 2rem); 1.25; balanced): followed by the world's divider ornament.
- **Event time** (display 600, clamp(2.4rem, 11vw, 3rem), 1, tabular): the hour of each ceremony.
- **Heading label** (display 600, 15px, 0.2em, uppercase, accent-text): event names ("Lễ Vu Quy", "Tiệc Cưới"); family headings use 17px at 0.12em.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.625, max 36 to 38ch, `text-wrap: pretty`): invitation message, hall copy, thank-you.
- **Body small** (14 to 15px, ink-soft): addresses, lunar short dates, notes, field labels (14px, 500).
- **Caption label** (13px, 0.16em uppercase in the ruled countdown; 13px plain under tiles): countdown units and the footer credit. Nothing on the card is set below 12px.

### Named Rules
**The Unbroken Name Rule.** A person's name never wraps mid-name: names up to 28 characters are set `white-space: nowrap`, and abbreviations bind to the next word with a no-break space (TP. Huế, Q. 3). Thumbnails size names in container units so they stay on one line at any card width.

**The Lunar Companion Rule.** A solar date on the card is always accompanied by its lunar date.

## Layout

One centered column. Outside the editor preview, the card is a single sheet, max 560px wide, on the ground with a 12px gutter and 16px top on phones (the card sits inset on its ground, never edge to edge) and 40px vertical margin from 640px up. Sections are centered, 24px side padding (40px from 640px), 56px vertical rhythm (40px for the compact family and countdown sections). Inner measures stay narrow: names at full card width, body copy 36 to 38ch, the date block 340px, countdown 360 to 380px, story timeline 420px, family pair 460px.

Section order is fixed by the story: cover (crown ornament, headline wording, photo in the world's frame, names, date block, lunar line), quote, families and "Trân trọng kính mời" + guest name, countdown, ceremonies (time, date, lunar, venue, address, map and calendar buttons), story timeline (title, then date, then text), album, 3D hall, RSVP, wishes, gift box, footer with the names in script and a share button. Sections switch on and off but never reorder.

Responsive changes are small: the family block goes two-column from 480px; gift amounts go four-across from 640px; the album is a two-column grid whose first photo spans both columns at 4:3, the rest at 3:4.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical rather than interface depth: the card is an object lifted off its table, and the envelope is a paper object with flaps. Within the card, surfaces are flat: panels are separated by a tone step (paper to panel) and a 1px line, never by shadow. Grain (opacity up to 0.55, multiply on light paper, screen on dark) gives the stock its tooth.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card lift** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 70px -30px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`): the card sheet on its ground.
- **Envelope lift** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px <line>88, 0 30px 60px -20px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`): the envelope body.
- **Seal** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 6px 10px rgba(0,0,0,0.35))`): the wax seal.
- **Soft press** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px -6px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`): primary buttons in soft-shape worlds only; gentle and square worlds set `--inv-btn-shadow: none`.
- **Floating toggle** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -6px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`): the fixed music button.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Sheet Rule.** Nothing on the card casts a shadow except soft-world buttons. The card and envelope are the only lifted objects.

## Shapes

Corner language is a world switch, exposed as `--inv-r` (panels, fields, tiles, choices) and `--inv-btn-r` (buttons):
- **soft** (12px panels, 999px pill buttons): sen-hong, mau-nuoc, hong-pastel.
- **gentle** (4px for both): song-hy-do, la-xanh, ren-co-dien, bien-xanh, do-do.
- **square** (0px for both, print rules): son-mai, toi-gian, baroque-vang, tap-chi.

The card takes `--inv-r` as its own corner. The printed **card frame** is the second switch: `none`; `hairline` (1px line, inset 10px); `double` (3px double line inset 8px plus a 1px dashed line inset 16px, lace); `gold-inset` (1.5px line inset 9px plus a 1px line at 55% inset 14px). The **photo frame** is the third: arch (999px top, 6 to 8px bottom), oval, circle, rect (2px), or full-bleed (magazine cover only), always with a 1px line border and 6px mat. The **countdown** is the fourth: `tiles` (four panel boxes at `--inv-r`) or `ruled` (numerals between top and bottom rules with vertical hairline separators).

## Components

### Envelope (signature)
Full-screen on the world's ground, portrait (min(88vw, 460px) by min(72vh, 620px)). Body, two side flaps (brightness 0.94 and 0.9), a bottom pocket (brightness 1.04) and a top flap whose underside shows the liner and a faint ornament divider. The wax seal (5.2rem) sits at the flap point: a scalloped wax shape in seal colour with an inner ring and either the drawn 囍 (song-hy and clouds worlds) or the couple's monogram in the script face. Address block near the bottom: "KÍNH MỜI" in the display face, 15px, 0.22em, then the guest's name (or "Quý khách") in script, clamp(1.9rem, 9vw, 2.6rem). Below the envelope, "Chạm vào thiệp để mở" at 16px.
- **Opening:** the flap rotates 180° on X (650ms, cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.2, 1)), the seal scales to 0.75 and fades, the inner card rises 70% (900ms, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 520ms delay), the screen fades (500ms, 1350ms delay), gone at 1900ms. Music starts on the same tap. Reduced motion skips straight to the card.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** `--inv-btn-r` (pill, 4px or 0 by world), min-height 48px.
- **Primary:** accent fill, on-accent text, 15px 600, 28px side padding, icon 16px with 8px gap. Hover brightness 1.1; active nudges 1px down; disabled 55% opacity.
- **Outline:** 1px accent border, ink text, 24px side padding, same height; used for "Lưu vào lịch" and "Chia sẻ thiệp".
- **Copy chip:** 40px high, 1px line border, 13px 600, accent border on hover.
- **Music toggle:** 48px accent circle fixed bottom-right, spinning note while playing.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** panel background, 1px line border, `--inv-r` corners, 12px by 16px padding, 16px text (prevents iOS zoom), placeholder in ink-soft at 80%. Labels above, 14px 500 ink-soft.
- **Focus:** border and 1px ring in accent; global `:focus-visible` is a 2px accent outline at 3px offset; caret in accent.
- **Error:** 14px 500 accent-text message with `role="alert"`, written as a friendly request ("Bạn nhập tên giúp mình nhé.").

### Choices
Two-up attendance radios (52px) and gift amount toggles (46px): idle is panel fill with line border, hover shifts the border to accent; selected is accent fill with on-accent text. Steppers are 44px circles with line borders.

### Cards / Containers
- **Card sheet:** paper, `--inv-r`, card lift shadow, grain, card frame.
- **Panels** (countdown tiles, wish cards, RSVP and gift confirmation): panel fill, 1px line border, no shadow.
- **Date block:** three cells; side cells ruled top and bottom in line colour.
- **Story timeline:** a 1px line spine with 15px ring markers (2px accent border on paper fill); title, then date in italic accent-text, then text.

### Ornaments
One drawn SVG family per world, in two variants: `crown` (above the names, 78 to 88% of the column, max 300 to 340px) and `divider` (under each section title and in the footer, about 240px). Strokes and fills use accent and line; worlds with fixed botanical or material colours (lotus pinks and leaf green, eucalyptus greens, watercolour rose washes, lacquer seal red, harbour gold) carry them inside the ornament.

### Card Face (thumbnail)
The front of a world at 3:4 for the gallery and editor picker: crown ornament, names on one line each, and a ruled date line, all sized in container units (`cqw`); tap-chi shows its masthead block with the initials. The card frame is approximated with border and inset shadow.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** add a world by adding one `InviteTheme` entry with all nine roles, envelope and petal colours, a script face, an ornament, `shape`, `cardFrame`, `countdown` and `frame`, and `accentText` whenever the accent misses 4.5:1 as text.
- **Do** read colours, corners and faces through `--inv-*` variables (`--inv-r`, `--inv-btn-r`, `--inv-btn-shadow`, `--inv-accent-text`) in every shared component.
- **Do** keep the envelope as the first viewport, addressed to the guest by name, with "Quý khách" when there is no name.
- **Do** set the lunar date beside every solar date and keep the countdown with the main date.
- **Do** keep taps at 44 to 48px and body at 16px, and load every face with the Vietnamese subset and `preload: false`.
- **Do** draw ornaments, the 囍 glyph and seals as inline SVG paths.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: the envelope opens instantly and petals are hidden.

### Don't:
